# Broad-market BTC research specification — 2026-09-08

Status: DATA/MEASUREMENT EXTENSION + RESEARCH PLAN, not a fitted/promoted predictor. Only BTC FINAL REPORT in the existing Google Sheet is user-facing. This file and all evidence are internal.

## Finding and design
The prior agent mostly described backward-looking hourly BTC price/flow, then repeatedly relabeled its near-term outlook. That is not evidence of forward accuracy. Cash market absorption, derivatives positions, financial conditions, monetary-policy surprises, institutional financing and supply events are different mechanisms and horizons. Separate: intraday execution risk (hours), directional forecast (7/30 days), macro regime (90/180/365 days). Intraday descriptions must not silently change a monthly forecast. No variable has an unconditional guaranteed sign. Prediction requires OOS incremental value, not a plausible story or correlation chart.

## What is implemented in this release
Nine independently labeled FRED context series, original-unit changes, same-observation-date funding spread, strict source identity/time/missingness checks, dated SEC Strategy capital-allocation packet, and post-commit evaluation of existing issued forecasts. The context uses public official pages and retains only the observations actually present. Longer changes/standardization are missing without sufficient history. No fabricated historical releases. The 14-row prospective packet and its Tuesday scorer remain frozen. SP500 is current context only: its old PIT-history exclusion is not revoked.

## Mechanisms and next acquisition stages
1. Monetary/funding: bank reserve balances, secured overnight rate relative to interest on reserves, NFCI/ANFCI, actual Fed policy decisions versus BEFORE-release futures/OIS expectations, real yields, credit spreads, Treasury cash and auction settlement calendars. Compare consistent balance-sheet definitions/dates. WALCL Wednesday level minus WTREGEN weekly average minus daily RRP is NOT a synchronized measure of global liquidity. Declining reserves alone is not a banking crisis. Breakeven inflation includes liquidity/risk premia.
2. Global risk and liquidity: Nasdaq/SPX, broad USD, real yields, oil, gold and JPY; later ECB/PBoC/BoJ monetary aggregates using release times/vintages and local-currency growth. Keep FX translation changes separate from monetary expansion. Never optimize a global-M2-to-BTC lag on the already-seen sample or assume a universal lag. Freshness is judged by release calendars, not hourly age of monthly data.
3. Institutions: complete ETF net creations/redemptions with actual publication times; separate underlying BTC units from AUM price appreciation and matched ETF/futures hedges. CFTC categories and Bank Participation Report are delayed aggregates, not named banks' intentions. 13F reports omit short positions and have filing lags; ETF holdings cannot identify net exposure, client assets, or anti-BTC strategy. Monitor bank funding/collateral access and formal OCC/Fed/FDIC/SEC rules, not narratives.
4. Strategy and other BTC treasury companies: SEC acceptance timestamps; gross and NET fundraising, amount actually allocated to BTC, BTC buys/sales, preferred dividends, interest, maturities and holder-put dates, unrestricted cash versus dedicated reserves, premium to BTC NAV with explicit denominator, share dilution and preferred prices/yields. Do not double-count dollars across separate cash buckets. Cost basis is not a margin call. An unrealized accounting loss is not cash paid. BTC Yield is not investor return or debt-service cash flow. No automatic 'never sells' assumption.
5. Derivatives: cross-venue native-unit OI AND USD OI (remove price-induced notional growth); perpetual funding separately from dated-futures annualized basis; CME category exposures, volume-weighted taker imbalances, options implied/realized volatility and risk reversal at matched expiry. Each long has a short: OI growth is not proof of directional leverage or of who will be liquidated. Long/short account ratios are not net market exposure. Dealer gamma sign requires a defensible position model, not option OI alone.
6. Crypto-native supply/demand: issuer-verified stablecoin circulating supply minus treasury/bridged duplicates, actual exchange netflows with entity-label/version uncertainty, Coinbase premium in matched currency/time, miner sales using disclosed transactions and operating economics, government/court/unlock distributions from official notices. A transfer into a custodian or exchange address is not a completed sale. Futures depth/order books are not liquidation maps.
7. Events: scheduled CPI/jobs/FOMC, auctions, option expiry, regulation/court decisions, hacks, war/energy shocks. Distinguish an event's announcement, effective date, cash-settlement date and forecast issue time. Unexpected news cannot be forecast by pretending it was observable earlier.

## Preregistered research process; no scoring in this release
Existing baseline contract remains development <=2021-12-31, validation 2022-2023, consumed 2024-2025, weekly stride and 7/30/90/180/365 horizons. Source families that did not exist before 2022 (e.g. US spot ETF flows) cannot be invented or assigned old validation wins. They need a separately frozen prospective source/feature experiment and later unseen outcomes.

Stage A is a strict source/PIT audit with no outcome-driven feature selection. Before ANY new model scores, commit a separate numerical manifest giving exact feature names, transformations, lags, model parameters, probability calibration, forecast issue/publication deadlines, target price/venue/time, execution rules if relevant, candidate count and missing-data rule. This document is not a substitute for that future numerical manifest. Stop that family if provenance is inadequate.

Stage B candidates are small, separate ablations: funding/liquidity; cross-asset risk; verified institutional net flows; Strategy financing; native supply; genuine-history derivatives; then only preregistered interactions. Reuse relevant zero-return/random-walk, constant-mean, momentum and frozen price-only comparators. An ensemble is eligible only if each added component has independent incremental evidence. Do not fit one large 'all factors' model and call higher training accuracy improvement.

Stage C compares exactly paired origins per horizon, with every training label s+h<=t and every observation release/capture<=t. Fit scaling, lag choice and any probability calibration using training data only. No shuffled splits. New redesigns use a later pristine prospective block starting only after their complete code/feature/source manifest and publication proof, never 2024-2025 or already-observed 2026. Original shadow thresholds (13 weekly 7d +3 nonoverlap30d for WATCH;26/6/3 for review plus original accuracy gates) are not lowered or auto-promoted.

Metrics: dollar price MAE/RMSE/median absolute error/signed bias; log-return and volatility-normalized error; rank IC; direction and balanced accuracy; Brier/reliability only for properly calibrated model+horizon; preregistered drawdown/risk event precision/recall and MAE/MFE. Show paired and non-overlap n; non-overlap is not proof of statistical independence. Use ordered block resampling/HAC for uncertainty, never random-shuffled training. Control multiple testing across ALL tried variants; retain failed sources/models. Prefer simpler models when differences are not distinguishable. Never optimize trading PnL before freezing fees, spreads, slippage and execution.

## Reporting/notification rules
Russian investor summary first, separate tested forecast from provisional explanations. Do not compute invented scenario probabilities or generic weighted economic score. Keep exact observation/publication/collection dates. A low-volatility sideways move can trigger alternating hourly inequalities: that alone is not a material monthly regime change. Record threshold versions at publication and assess against that stored version; never move a rolling low after a breach and erase the breach. New alert persistence/materiality thresholds require preregistration, not hindsight tuning.

## Primary research and rules
- IMF WP 2023/163, The Crypto Cycle and US Monetary Policy: https://www.imf.org/en/publications/wp/issues/2023/08/04/the-crypto-cycle-and-us-monetary-policy-534834 . Empirical monetary/risk linkage, not guaranteed local prediction.
- BIS WP1087, Crypto carry: https://www.bis.org/publications/working-paper-1087-crypto-carry . Carry reflects segmentation/risk and arbitrage constraints; hedge shorts are not necessarily bearish bets.
- Federal Reserve balance-sheet mechanics: https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/November-2025-Federal-Reserve-Balance-Sheet-Developments.htm . Read current policy rather than assume perpetual QT.
- NY Fed reserve demand: https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/reserve-demand-elasticity . Funding pressure/reserve scarcity distinct from a reserve level.
- SEC Form13F FAQ: https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/division-investment-management-frequently-asked-questions/frequently-asked-questions-about-form-13f . Short positions excluded; reporting delay.
- CFTC COT: https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/CommitmentsofTraders/index.htm . Tuesday positions generally Friday publication; use actual holiday release schedule. Category is not intent.
- OCC permitted bank custody/execution: https://www.occ.gov/news-issuances/news-releases/2025/nr-occ-2025-42.html . Client service is not proprietary exposure.
- Strategy official results: https://www.strategy.com/press/strategy-announces-second-quarter-2026-financial-results_07-30-2026 . BTC sales and capital management are possible; do not presume permanent one-way demand.
- Strategy SEC current disclosure: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1050446/000119312526384402/mstr-20260831.htm . Sep8 disclosure: no BTC buys/sales Aug31-Sep7; STRC repurchases $176.3m from USD Cash. Dated fact, not future purchase promise.
